Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Chicken Merge (hereinafter referred to as "the App" or "we"). We take your privacy protection and personal information security very seriously. This Privacy Policy aims to explain how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information, as well as your related rights.

Before using the App, please carefully read and fully understand this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this Privacy Policy, please stop using the App immediately.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

When you use the App, we may collect the following information:

  • Game progress and achievement data
  • In-app purchase records
  • Feedback and suggestions you voluntarily provide

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

To improve gaming experience and service quality, we may automatically collect the following information:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version, device identifiers)
  • Game usage data (play time, level progress, in-game behavior)
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • Network information (IP address, network type)

3. How We Use Your Information

We collect and use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Provide Game Services: Save your game progress and provide personalized gaming experience
  • Improve Products: Analyze game data to optimize game performance and user experience
  • Customer Support: Respond to your inquiries and feedback, resolve technical issues
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and illegal activities
  • Advertising Services: Display relevant ads through third-party advertising platforms (if applicable)

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We promise not to sell your personal information. We only share your information in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Share with third-party service providers who help us operate the App (such as cloud storage, data analytics, advertising platforms)
  • Legal Requirements: As required by laws, legal processes, litigation, or mandatory government requirements
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale
  • With Consent: In other circumstances with your explicit consent

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services: For app distribution and updates
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements (if applicable)
  • Google Analytics: For data analysis (if applicable)

These third-party services have their own privacy policies. We recommend you review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Privacy Policy

6. Children's Privacy Protection

We take children's privacy protection very seriously. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you are a parent or guardian and discover that your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us, and we will promptly delete the relevant information.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your personal information security, including:

  • Data encryption during transmission and storage
  • Access control and permission management
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability fixes

However, please note that no method of Internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure.

8. Data Retention

We only retain your personal information for the period necessary to achieve the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. When you delete the App or request data deletion, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, unless we are required by law to retain it.

9. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Right to Erasure: You have the right to request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Restriction: You have the right to request restriction of processing your personal information
  • Right to Object: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal information
  • Right to Data Portability: You have the right to receive your personal information in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format

To exercise the above rights, please contact us using the contact information below.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed on servers outside your country or region. These countries or regions may have data protection laws different from those in your country or region. We will take appropriate measures to ensure your personal information is adequately protected.

11. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you within the App or through other appropriate means. We recommend you regularly review this page to stay informed of the latest Privacy Policy.

Any changes to this Privacy Policy will take effect immediately upon posting. Your continued use of the App after changes indicates your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
